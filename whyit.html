MY GITHUB

I chose to study IT because I took an introductory programming course in high school and really enjoyed it. Although my programming knowledge and skills were relatively and are still very very limited, the experience alongside my interest for computer hardware made me interested in studying IT.

During middle school and high school, I also spent a lot of time working with personal computers. I built my first PC when I was 15 years old with money Id saved over holiday savings because I wanted to be able to run the video games I played at higher framerates, this alongside watching lots of youtubers like Linus Tech Tips eventually led to me developing an interest in computer hardware. During this period, I also tried to flip laptops by buying seemingly broken ones and attempting to repair them. Unfortunately, these attempts were unsuccessful, but they gave me experience troubleshooting hardware and showed me how fun it is to fix things in general especially personal computers.

One of the biggest reasons I enjoy IT as a whole is that in general I really like the idea of autonomy and personalization EG: having my own programs tailored to me specifically and my own computer, the idea of tailoring tech whether its hardware or software to my own personal life is very appealing to me in general.
